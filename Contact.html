About Me

Education

Seyed Alireza Mousavian Anaraki started PhD studies in Data Science at university of Rome Tor Vergata, Italy in 2023. He held his MSc degree in Industrial Engineering (Engineering Management) from Iran University of Science and Technology, Iran in 2021. Moreover, He held his BSc degree in Industrial Engineering from the University of Kashan, Iran in 2019 (Ranked 1st student in both degrees).

Experience

He was the teacher assistant in the third semester in the Linear Algebra course at the undergraduate level. After holding the BSc degree, he worked as a data analyst and market research at Nimo Engine Company. He has recently worked in a data mining research group under Iran's National Elites Foundation (INEF) supervision as a data-driven reliability-centered maintenance specialist to improve the maintenance performance of gas transmission networks. He currently works as a strategic planning and performance evaluation specialist at Sepahan Battery Industrial Complex. Furthermore he is business development consultant of Karo Behin Sanat Company.

Research interests

Data-driven decision making, Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, and Machine Learning, Natural language processing, Operation Research Applications, Business analytics & Strategic planning.
