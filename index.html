About Me

Education & Experience

Seyed Alireza Mousavian Anaraki is a PhD candidate in Data Science at the University of Rome Tor Vergata, Italy, where he started his doctoral studies in 2023. His research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP) for sustainability (ESG) analysis, particularly on large-scale processing of sustainability reports, weak-supervision techniques, and the application of Large Language Models (LLMs) for structure, consistency, explainability, and credibility assessment.

In 2026, he joined the School of Computer Science at the University of Sheffield, UK, as a Visiting Researcher under the supervision of Dr. Nafise Sadat Moosavi. During this research visit, he investigated credibility bias in ESG language models, studying how geographic, institutional, and linguistic factors influence automated sustainability assessment. This work explored approaches for auditing contextual influence in LLM-based ESG evaluation by examining whether model decisions are driven by sustainability evidence or by associated contextual signals. The research involved NLP and LLM-based methods for ESG reporting, including model interpretability, credibility assessment, and the analysis of contextual effects on sustainability-related language understanding.

He earned his MSc in Industrial Engineering (Engineering Management) from the Iran University of Science and Technology, Iran, in 2021, where his research focused on balanced clustering, automatic labeling, and ensemble learning for decision-making systems. He also received his BSc degree in Industrial Engineering from the University of Kashan, Iran, in 2019, where he was ranked the first student in both degrees.

His previous experience includes serving as a teaching assistant for the undergraduate Linear Algebra course. After completing his BSc, he worked as a data analyst and market researcher at Nimo Engine Company. He later joined a data mining research group under the supervision of Iran's National Elites Foundation (INEF), where he worked as a data-driven reliability-centered maintenance researcher to improve maintenance performance in gas transmission networks. He has also worked as a strategic planning and performance evaluation specialist at Sepahan Battery Industrial Complex and as a business development consultant for Karo Behin Sanat Company.
