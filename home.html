About Me

Education

Seyed Alireza Mousavian Anaraki started PhD studies in Data Science at the University of Rome Tor Vergata, Italy, in 2023. His research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP) for sustainability (ESG) analysis, large-scale report processing, weak supervision, and large language models (LLMs). He earned his MSc in Industrial Engineering from Iran University of Science and Technology in 2021, and his BSc from the University of Kashan in 2019 (ranked first in both degrees).

Experience

He was a teaching assistant in Linear Algebra. He worked as a data analyst at Nimo Engine Company, then as a researcher under Iran's National Elites Foundation on maintenance optimization for gas networks. He later worked in strategic planning at Sepahan Battery Industrial Complex and as a business consultant.

Research interests

(i) Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Machine Learning, (ii) AI-driven decision-making, (iii) NLP and LLMs for ESG, (iv) Operations research.
