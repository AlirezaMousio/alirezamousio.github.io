About Me

Education

Seyed Alireza Mousavian Anaraki started PhD studies in Data Science at the University of Rome Tor Vergata, Italy, in 2023. His research focuses on Natural Language Processing (NLP) for sustainability (ESG) analysis, particularly on large-scale processing of sustainability reports, weak-supervision techniques, and the use of large language models (LLMs) for structure, consistency, and credibility assessment. He also earned his MSc in Industrial Engineering (Engineering Management) from the Iran University of Science and Technology, Iran, in 2021, focusing on balanced clustering, automatic labeling, and ensemble learning for decision-making systems. Moreover, he earned his BSc degree in Industrial Engineering from the University of Kashan, Iran, in 2019 (he was ranked the first student in both degrees).

Experience

He was the teaching assistant in the third semester of the Linear Algebra course at the undergraduate level. After earning a BSc degree, he worked as a data analyst and market researcher at Nimo Engine Company. Then, he worked in a data mining research group under Iran's National Elites Foundation (INEF) supervision as a data-driven reliability-centered maintenance specialist to improve the maintenance performance of gas transmission networks. He has recently worked as a strategic planning and performance evaluation specialist at Sepahan Battery Industrial Complex. Furthermore, he was a business development consultant for Karo Behin Sanat Company.

Research interests

  • Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Machine Learning
  • AI-driven decision-making
  • NLP and LLMs for ESG
  • Operations research
